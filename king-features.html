King Features (Hearst)

Reimagining comics for a digital-first audience

Product Strategy Intern
2023
Figma, Customer Research, Prototyping

Overview

As a Product Strategy Intern at King Features (Hearst), I led the digital transformation initiative for classic print comics, focusing on engaging younger, mobile-first readers while creating new revenue streams for creators.

Challenge

Classic print comics needed a digital transformation to engage younger, mobile-first readers while building new revenue streams for creators. The traditional comic format wasn't optimized for mobile consumption, and there was a disconnect between legacy content and modern digital habits.

Key Problems

  • • Print-first format didn't translate well to mobile screens
  • • Limited discovery mechanisms for new readers
  • • Lack of monetization opportunities for digital creators
  • • Disconnect between legacy content and modern consumption habits

My Approach

1. Customer Research

Conducted 50+ customer interviews to gather insights on digital comic consumption habits, preferences, and pain points.

  • → Identified mobile-first reading preferences
  • → Discovered social sharing and discovery needs
  • → Validated subscription vs. pay-per-issue models

2. Product Design

Designed an interactive comic app prototype in Figma with modern discovery and reading features optimized for mobile consumption.

  • → Panel-by-panel reading experience for mobile
  • → AI-powered content recommendation engine
  • → Social features for sharing and discussion

3. Business Strategy

Developed a comprehensive go-to-market plan including syndication strategy, pricing models, and creator monetization framework.

  • → Tiered subscription model with premium content
  • → Creator revenue sharing and incentive programs
  • → Strategic partnerships with digital platforms

Impact

$2M+

Projected new annual revenue from prototype and business model

50+

Customer interviews conducted to validate digital-first opportunities

The prototype and strategic recommendations validated digital-first opportunities that influenced the strategic direction for Hearst's comics portfolio. The executive presentation highlighted key market opportunities and provided a roadmap for digital transformation.

Executive Presentation

Pitched comprehensive strategy directly to Hearst executives, presenting:

  • • Market analysis and competitive landscape
  • • User research insights and persona development
  • • Product prototype and user experience design
  • • Revenue projections and monetization strategy
  • • Implementation roadmap and success metrics
