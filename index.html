About me

Hi, I'm Takuto.

I'm a product builder from Japan, based in Los Angeles — a USC Marshall student in Business Administration with a minor in AI Application & Product Design.

My path has always been about connecting people, ideas, and communities: product internships at Snap, Hearst, and Warner Bros. Discovery; startups like BizLang and Homie; and communities like ProductSC and FEMO. The common thread is building experiences that bring people closer together — and closer to what's next for them.
