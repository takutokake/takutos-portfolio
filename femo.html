FEMO

Connecting students across cultures through English mentorship

Founder
2021–Present
Nonprofit Initiative

Overview

As the founder of FEMO (Foreign English Mentorship Organization), I created a nonprofit initiative that connects students across cultures through English mentorship, fostering cross-cultural relationships and improving communication skills for international students.

Challenge

International students often lack opportunities for conversational English practice with supportive peers. Traditional language learning resources focus on grammar and vocabulary but don't provide the cultural context and real-world conversation practice needed for academic and social success.

Key Problems

  • • Limited conversational English practice opportunities
  • • Cultural barriers preventing natural peer connections
  • • Lack of supportive mentorship for international students
  • • Academic and social integration challenges

My Approach

1. Mentorship Program Design

Founded FEMO to pair mentors and mentees across cultures, creating structured yet flexible relationships focused on language development and cultural exchange.

  • → One-on-one mentor-mentee pairing system
  • → Structured conversation topics and cultural exchange activities
  • → Flexible scheduling to accommodate different time zones and commitments

2. Program Development

Organized comprehensive mentorship programs, workshops, and cultural exchange sessions to create meaningful learning experiences.

  • → Weekly mentorship sessions with conversation practice
  • → Group workshops on academic English and cultural norms
  • → Cultural exchange events and community building activities

3. International Network Building

Built an international network of volunteers to sustain operations and expand impact across different regions and educational institutions.

  • → Volunteer recruitment and training programs
  • → Cross-cultural mentor training and support systems
  • → Sustainable organizational structure for long-term impact

Supported dozens of students in improving their communication skills, with many reporting increased confidence in academic and social settings.
