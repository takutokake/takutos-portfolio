ProductSC

Rebuilt from 5 members to 200+, creating USC's hub for aspiring PMs

President
2024–Present
USC's only Product Management Club

Overview

As President of ProductSC, I transformed USC's only Product Management club from a struggling 5-member organization into a thriving 200+ member community that serves as the university's central hub for aspiring product managers.

Challenge

USC lacked a central community and educational space for product management. Students interested in PM had no structured way to learn fundamental skills, connect with peers, or gain practical experience. The existing club was dormant with minimal engagement and no clear educational framework.

Key Problems

  • • No structured PM education or curriculum at USC
  • • Lack of community for aspiring product managers
  • • Limited practical experience opportunities
  • • Dormant club with only 5 inactive members

My Approach

1. Curriculum Development

Created USC's first foundational PM curriculum with structured learning paths for different experience levels and career goals.

  • → Compass: Introductory curriculum for PM fundamentals
  • → Catalyst: Recruitment-focused curriculum for job preparation
  • → Client Projects: Real-world PM work with external partners

2. Community Building

Led 60+ active members through workshops, case competitions, and alumni engagement events to create a vibrant PM community at USC.

  • → Weekly workshops on PM skills and industry trends
  • → Case competitions with real product challenges
  • → Alumni networking events and mentorship programs

3. Industry Partnerships

Secured strategic partnerships with leading tech companies to provide students with real-world exposure and career opportunities.

  • → Partnership with Riot Games for exclusive workshops
  • → Guest speaker series with industry PM leaders
  • → Recruitment pipeline for internship and full-time opportunities

Educational Impact

Created USC's first foundational PM curriculum, shaping how students explore and enter the product management field.

  • • Practical experience through client projects and case studies
  • • Career preparation and recruitment support

Community Legacy

Established a sustainable organization that continues to grow and serve as USC's central hub for product management education and community building.
