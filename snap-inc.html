Snap Inc

Exploring the intersection of AR and sports entertainment

AR Product Extern
2023
Lens Studio, AR Design

Overview

As an AR Product Extern at Snap Inc, I explored how Snapchat could leverage AR lenses to create new, interactive experiences within sports entertainment, focusing on fan engagement and participation.

Challenge

How can Snapchat use AR lenses to engage fans in new, interactive ways within sports? The challenge was to identify opportunities where AR could enhance the sports viewing experience and create deeper fan participation beyond traditional social media engagement.

Key Opportunities

  • • Interactive fan experiences during live events
  • • Gamification of sports viewing through AR
  • • Social sharing and community building around sports
  • • Bridging physical and digital sports experiences

My Approach

1. AR Lens Development

Designed and launched a sports-themed AR Lens on Snapchat, inspired by horse racing, to explore interactive sports entertainment possibilities.

  • → Utilized Lens Studio for rapid prototyping and iteration
  • → Implemented interactive elements and gamification
  • → Optimized for mobile performance and user experience

2. Product Vision Development

Developed comprehensive product vision for AR in sports entertainment, identifying key use cases and market opportunities.

  • → Market research on AR adoption in sports
  • → User journey mapping for sports fans
  • → Technical feasibility assessment

3. Executive Presentation

Presented product vision and execution strategy to Snap executives, demonstrating the potential for AR in sports entertainment.

  • → Live demo of working AR lens
  • → Strategic roadmap for sports partnerships
  • → Revenue and engagement projections

Impact

Lens Performance

The sports-themed AR lens gained strong engagement from target audiences, validating the concept of AR-enhanced sports entertainment.

  • • High user engagement and retention rates
  • • Positive user feedback on interactive elements
  • • Successful technical implementation and performance

Strategic Insights

The project highlighted significant opportunities for AR to enhance sports entertainment and fan participation, informing future product development strategies.

  • • Identified key partnership opportunities with sports leagues
  • • Validated market demand for interactive sports content
  • • Demonstrated technical feasibility of complex AR experiences
