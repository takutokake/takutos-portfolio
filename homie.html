Building a social app to make meeting new friends feel natural and fun

Firebase, React, Figma

Overview

As the founder of Homie, I'm building a social platform that makes meeting new friends feel natural and fun through curated group hangouts called "Drops." The app focuses on second-degree connections and group-based social discovery as an alternative to traditional dating and event apps.

Challenge

College students and new grads struggle to form genuine connections in today's digital landscape. Dating apps feel cringe for platonic relationships, and traditional event apps lack follow-through, leaving people feeling disconnected despite being surrounded by potential friends.

Key Problems

  • • Dating apps create awkward dynamics for platonic friendships
  • • Event apps have poor follow-through and shallow connections
  • • Cold approaches to strangers feel unnatural and forced
  • • Lack of trust and safety in meeting new people

My Approach

1. "Drops" - Curated Hangouts

Created a platform for themed meetups announced weekly, focusing on shared interests and activities rather than individual profiles.

  • → Weekly themed hangouts (coffee chats, hiking, study groups)
  • → Small group sizes (4-8 people) for meaningful connections
  • → Activity-focused rather than profile-focused interactions

2. Referral-Based Onboarding

Built a "homies bringing homies" system focusing on second-degree connections to create trust and natural social dynamics.

  • → Invite-only system through existing users
  • → Mutual connections create natural conversation starters
  • → Built-in trust through friend-of-friend relationships

3. Technical Development

Developed comprehensive product from concept to prototype using modern web technologies and design thinking principles.

  • → Figma mockups and user experience design
  • → Firebase backend for real-time data and authentication
  • → React-based prototype with responsive design

User Validation

Pilot events highlighted strong interest in group-based social discovery, with participants expressing preference for activity-focused meetups over traditional social apps.

  • • High engagement and return participation rates
  • • Organic friend connections formed beyond initial events
  • • Positive feedback on referral-based trust system

Market Positioning

Successfully positioned Homie as a differentiated alternative to generic social apps, focusing on authentic connections through shared experiences rather than superficial matching algorithms.
